Our story

The Cameroon Cancer Foundation was founded and registered in 2012. Its work is built around sensitization and education, screening, treatment assistance, capacity building and research. Since 2019, CCF has worked closely with the Cameroon Oncology Center to connect community cancer-control activities with specialist diagnosis and treatment.

“Cancer is a universal nuisance; when it touches one person, it affects everybody.”

Notre histoire

La Cameroon Cancer Foundation a été fondée et enregistrée en 2012. Son action repose sur la sensibilisation et l'éducation, le dépistage, l'aide au traitement, le renforcement des capacités et la recherche. Depuis 2019, la CCF travaille étroitement avec le Cameroon Oncology Center afin de relier les activités communautaires de lutte contre le cancer au diagnostic et au traitement spécialisés.

« Le cancer est un fléau universel ; lorsqu'il touche une personne, il affecte tout le monde. »

Professor Paul N. Mobit

Medical physicist, radiation oncology professional, educator, researcher and healthcare entrepreneur with more than three decades of cancer-care experience. Professor Mobit founded the Cameroon Cancer Foundation and Cameroon Oncology Center and has focused his work on expanding safe, evidence-based cancer care in Cameroon.
